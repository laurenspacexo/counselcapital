Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Counsel Capital Group is committed to making our website and information about our global business support services accessible to all visitors.

Our commitment

We aim to provide a website that can be used by the widest possible audience, regardless of ability, technology, or location. As we support businesses operating across markets, we believe clear and inclusive digital communication is essential.

We work toward conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and review our website as it develops to improve usability and accessibility.

Accessibility measures on this site

  • Semantic HTML, clear landmark regions, and a logical heading structure.
  • A skip-to-content link for keyboard and screen-reader users.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus states for interactive elements.
  • Text and background color choices designed for readable contrast.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images and labels for navigation icons.
  • Visible, programmatically associated labels for form fields.
  • A responsive layout designed to remain usable at increased zoom levels and across devices.
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences where animations are used.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We review our site as content and features are added, and we use feedback to identify and address barriers. Some third-party content or tools may not be fully within our control, but we will work to provide an accessible alternative where reasonably possible.

Need help or want to report a barrier?

If you experience difficulty using this website or need information in another format, please contact us at accessibility@counselcapitalgroup.com or through our contact page. Please include the page or feature you were using and a brief description of the issue. We aim to acknowledge accessibility feedback within two business days.
