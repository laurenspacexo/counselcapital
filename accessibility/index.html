Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Counsel Capital Group is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all visitors, including people with disabilities.

Our commitment

Counsel Capital Group is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability. We are actively working to conform this website to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and to meet our obligations under Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and equivalent standards in the jurisdictions in which we operate, including the European Accessibility Act.

Measures we have taken

  • Semantic HTML structure with landmark regions and a logical heading order.
  • A skip-to-content link on every page for keyboard and screen reader users.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators on all interactive elements.
  • Text and background color combinations that meet WCAG AA contrast ratios.
  • Descriptive alternative text and accessible labels for non-text content and icons.
  • Form fields with visible, programmatically associated labels and clear error identification.
  • Responsive layout that supports zoom up to 200% without loss of content or function.
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences set at the operating system level.

Ongoing efforts

Digital accessibility is an ongoing effort. We periodically review this website using both automated evaluation tools and manual testing, including keyboard-only navigation and screen reader testing, and we update our practices as standards and technology evolve.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, please let us know so we can address it. You can reach our team at accessibility@counselcapitalgroup.com or through our contact page. We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within two business days.
