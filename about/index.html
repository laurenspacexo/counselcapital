About Counsel Capital Group

Advisors in business strategy consultation, legal operations, and compliance.

Counsel Capital Group was built on a simple premise: organizations make their most consequential decisions at the intersection of business strategy, legal operations, and regulatory obligation - and that intersection deserves advisors who take all three seriously, at once.

Our Mission

Counsel that operates, not just opines.

We were founded to close a gap we saw repeatedly: firms that could issue a legal opinion but could not help implement it, and consultancies that could redesign a process but could not account for the regulatory exposure it created. Counsel Capital Group exists to give clients both grounded and operational advice that holds up to legal and regulatory scrutiny.

Our work spans three core disciplines. In business consultation, we advise leadership and boards on governance structure, operating models, and the strategic decisions where commercial and legal risk are inseparable. In legal operations, we help corporate legal departments and outside counsel run more efficiently - matter management, budgeting, vendor and technology strategy, and workflow design. In regulatory compliance, we build and review programs across jurisdictions, from accessibility obligations to industry-specific regulatory regimes, so that our clients are prepared before a regulator or plaintiff ever asks.

What sets our practice apart

  • Engagements are led by senior advisors, not delegated to junior staffs.
  • Every recommendation is delivered in writing, with a defined implementation path.
  • We coordinate legal, operational, and compliance perspectives within a single engagement team.
  • Our advisory work is designed to withstand outside scrutiny - from a board, an auditor, or a regulator.
Where We Focus

Business consultation, legal operations, and compliance - in depth

These three disciplines make up the substance of our practice. Everything else we do supports them.

Business Consultation

Governance structure and board advisory, entity formation and restructuring, operating model design, risk-adjusted decision frameworks, and strategic advisory for organizations navigating growth, transition, or dispute.

Legal Operations

Legal department design and staffing strategy, matter and vendor management, budgeting and spend analysis, legal technology selection, workflow and intake process design, and outside counsel management frameworks.

Regulatory Compliance

Compliance program design and gap assessment, accessibility and ADA/WCAG conformance review, policy drafting and implementation, regulatory change monitoring, and remediation planning across jurisdictions.

How We Operate

Principles that govern every engagement

Rigor

We do not advise on assumptions. Every recommendation is grounded in a documented review of the facts, the applicable regulatory framework, and the operational reality on the ground.

Discretion

Compliance and legal operations matters are frequently sensitive. We treat client information with the confidentiality standard our clients would expect from outside counsel.

Accountability

We do not disappear after a recommendation is delivered. Our engagements include follow-through, so that compliance programs and operational changes are actually implemented.
