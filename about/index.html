About Counsel Capital Group

A practical partner for businesses operating across borders.

We help businesses secure the goods they need, turn market opportunities into growth, and keep essential commercial and legal matters organized as they scale.

Our Mission

Make global business easier to manage.

Counsel Capital Group was built for leaders who need more than one-off advice. When procurement, sales, logistics, contracts, and business decisions pull in different directions, we help bring them into a clear, coordinated plan.

Our work connects three critical areas of business. We support global procurement and logistics by helping clients source goods, evaluate suppliers, coordinate purchasing, and manage delivery. Through sales and business growth advisory, we help teams strengthen their commercial strategy and pursue new opportunities. With legal and commercial matter coordination, we help keep contracts, documentation, compliance priorities, and outside legal support organized around the needs of the business.

What clients can expect

  • A clear view of priorities across sourcing, growth, and business administration.
  • Practical recommendations that can be acted on by your team.
  • Coordinated support across suppliers, partners, and professional advisors.
  • A relationship focused on execution—not just reports or introductions.
