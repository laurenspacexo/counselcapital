Our Services

Advisory built around three disciplines.

Each engagement draws on business consultation, legal operations, and regulatory compliance in the proportion the matter actually requires - not a fixed package.

01 · Business Consultation

Strategic advisory where commercial and legal risk meet

We advise leadership teams and boards on the structural decisions that shape an organization's exposure - how it is governed, how it is capitalized, and how it responds under pressure.

  • GovernanceBoard structure, decision rights, and oversight design.
  • Entity StrategyFormation, restructuring, and multi-jurisdiction entity planning.
  • Risk-Adjusted PlanningDecision frameworks that price legal and regulatory exposure into strategy.
  • Transition AdvisoryLeadership change, M&A readiness, and dispute-adjacent strategy.
02 · Legal Operations

Making the legal function run like the rest of the business

We help corporate legal departments and outside counsel operate with the same discipline expected of finance or operations - measurable, budgeted, and well-instrumented.

  • Department DesignStaffing models, reporting lines, and org structure for corporate and legal teams.
  • Matter & Vendor ManagementOutside counsel selection, budgeting, and spend analysis.
  • Technology & WorkflowLegal technology selection and intake or workflow process design.
  • Performance ReportingMetrics and reporting frameworks for legal department leadership.
03 · Regulatory Compliance

Programs built to withstand scrutiny, not merely pass it

We design, review, and remediate compliance programs across jurisdictions - so our clients are prepared before a regulator, auditor, or plaintiff raises the question.

  • Program DesignCompliance frameworks tailored to sector and jurisdiction.
  • Accessibility ReviewADA Title III and WCAG conformance assessment and remediation planning.
  • Policy & ImplementationPolicy drafting, staff training design, and rollout support.
  • Regulatory MonitoringOngoing tracking of regulatory change relevant to the client's operations.
How an Engagement Begins

A structured, three-step process

01

Initial consultation

We meet to understand the matter, the stakeholders involved, and the outcome you need - typically within one week of first contact.

02

Scoped engagement proposal

You receive a written scope, timeline, and fee structure before any advisory work begins.

03

Advisory delivery and follow-through

Findings and recommendations are delivered in writing, with a defined path to implementation and a review cadence.

Tell us what you're deciding. We'll tell you what to consider before you decide it.
